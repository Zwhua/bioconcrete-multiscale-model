# Best Model Summary Draft (150 words)

BioConcrete links repair-agent release, environment-dependent activity,
carbonate chemistry, mineral deposition, transport and crack geometry to
measurable engineering outcomes. The model changed our experimental priority:
the uncalibrated baseline predicts only about 2.08% closure, almost entirely
from the assumed C-S-H payload, while predicted calcite contributes very
little. Increasing aggregate activity alone therefore has low expected value.
Instead, the model prioritizes matched complete-system, no-C-S-H and abiotic
C-S-H controls with simultaneous crack-width, calcite, substrate, calcium and
oxygen measurements. V0.5.0 identifies limitations through counterfactual
model responses rather than input-based rules and selects complementary
experiments using numerical sensitivities and Fisher information. It also
separates sensitivity, identifiability and prior uncertainty, preserves mass
balance across 0D, 1D and 2D models, and records evidence provenance for every
formal result. The team currently has no wet-lab time series and public
calibration remains incomplete, so all current design outputs are explicitly
uncalibrated prospective model predictions awaiting falsification.
